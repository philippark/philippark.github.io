So, I have this tendency of starting projects and never finishing them. Probably because I am way too self-critical to be a functioning human being. Sometimes I think I am so dumb and have accomplished nothing in life, yet whenever I look back at my previous work and achievements I just feel so astonished by the intelligence that beams out of it. 

Now I'm not saying I'm highly gifted. Above average intelligence maybe. It's just sometimes I feel like I don't even know how smart I even am. But if being proud of your previous work is a sign of something, perhaps it's a sign to be more proud of yourself. 

Which is why regular reflection is necessary, especially if you are someone like me who is self-critical and quits easily. Look back and see the good you have done, be astonished by it, and let that motivate you to keep going and surprise yourself even more. 

----

Which is why I am starting up my blog again. Apologies for the personal tangent, although I'm including it because 1. this is really just a stream of consiousness type of post and 2. maybe it could help someone else in some way. at the very least, help someone similar to me but just starting out, and also perhaps people can see that outward accomplishments don't always tell the full story. 

anyways.

I hope to use this platform to do a couple of things.

1. be a place where I can post my essays and thoughts.

- Writing is a really great way to consolidate your learnings. Reading is just one part of learning, you must be able to take what you learned, and write it out to really understand it. The great intellectuals of our present and past have all been essayists for a reason. Writing makes you really confront what you actually know, and most of time you realize it's hard to write because you don't actually know it well enough. But that's EXACTLY why you write. That struggling of trying to explain your thoughts makes you research more into it, and so as a challenge, I will be attempting to write an essay a month on a topic that I've learned. 

2. be a place where I can document my journey in a more polished and publicized way.

- I write all the time, but most of it is in my private journals, google docs, obsidian, and half finished notebooks. I'd like some of my writing to see the light of day, and a personal blog is a great way to do so. 


and uh, past me would maybe try to write to impress recruiters and land a job. But I already have a job now. So now I do this for me, which is how creation should be. 


And, it's also a great escape from AI. I will absolutely refuse to use AI for my writings, it defeats the whole point.
